Vaccination · Infection · Immunity

Hybrid immunity in calendar time

Does a booster before your first Omicron infection protect you — or imprint you? VVI answers with an exact, sampling-free hazard model. Below, every number comes from synthetic cohorts, fitted live by the package.

I · Plant an effect, then find it

Choose the truth. Watch the model recover it.

Each scenario is a cohort simulated by vvi.simulate, collapsed to its sufficient statistics by vvi.aggregate, and fitted by vvi.fit. You choose the planted imprinting effect — the true hazard ratio of reinfection for three Comirnaty doses before infection (BBBI) versus two (BBI) — and the cohort size.

II · How big a cohort?

Sampling throws away the effects you came for.

For a random sub-sample, the standard error of a log hazard ratio scales as 1/√n, so one fit gives the power of the imprinting test at any cohort size (vvi.power). Drag the true effect: a 300k sub-sample sees large effects; only the full cohort sees small ones.

III · Cost

Same likelihood, a table that does not grow.

The likelihood depends on the data only through people at risk and events per covariate cell and calendar day. A binomial GLM on those counts has the same estimates and standard errors as the person-day model — on at most cells × days rows. Hover the points.
